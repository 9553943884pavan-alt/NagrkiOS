# Phase 5 provisional authorship, label and language audit

All counts are **machine suggestions**, not verified labels. Of 1,000 screened records, the simple voice/staff cues returned 561 `citizen_candidate`, 3 `staff_note` and 436 `uncertain`; citizen candidates are not human-verified authorship. Quality flags include 434 with no first-person/request cue, 7 with staff vocabulary, 3 with conflicting pilot topics between title and description, and 1 with a possible corruption cue. These flags overlap and can be wrong.

Static pilot keywords suggested Roads for 486 records, Garbage for 144 and neither for 370. Across the full taxonomy, 232 suggestions differed from the publisher category; this is a **review trigger, not 232 proven publisher errors**. A road word may merely describe where a garbage complaint occurred. Both road and garbage words appeared in 83 records. One record has no meaningful publisher category (`NULL` sentinel), and no replacement label was assigned. Original source labels stay separate from suggestions.

For the two proposed pilot classes, publisher counts remain **340 Roads** and **245 Garbage**. The keyword rule matched the source label in 269 Roads and 129 Garbage cases; it suggested the opposite pilot label in 1 Roads and 13 Garbage cases. These counts do not measure accuracy because there is no human-reviewed truth set. The remaining pilot records had no unique pilot keyword or mixed evidence. Do not expand the pilot to the other 29 named source classes on this evidence.

All 1,000 source records have a Latin-script hint, **not verified English**. Local lexical cues yielded 926 `english_hint`, 2 `hinglish_hint` and 72 `latin_undetermined`, each with `machine_predicted` provenance. Verified language counts remain zero. Exact/number-template, high token-overlap and weak same-title/date cross-language checks found **0 duplicate candidate rows and 0 cross-split duplicate candidates** at their thresholds. This does not rule out semantic duplicates, translated variants or shared incidents; human reviewers must inspect suspected relationships. The 676/157/167 frozen split was not changed.
