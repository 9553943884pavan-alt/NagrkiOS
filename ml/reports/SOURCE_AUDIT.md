## Licensing and privacy conditions

- NYC Open Data is publicly reusable, but the [portal policy](https://a860-gpp.nyc.gov/downloads/5712m8144?locale=en) disclaims fitness and permits updates/corrections. Pin query, collection time, response checksum and selected fields for reproducibility.
- Mendeley CC BY applies to the published dataset, but its tweet content includes personal metadata and may have independent platform rights. No raw social-media corpus should be committed or sent to an external model service during this phase.
- BBMP's repository-level ODbL statement is not a substitute for verifying rights in BBMP-authored contents. Its staff-name field should not enter training text.
- The [Government Open Data License–India](https://data.gov.in/Godl) covers published open government resources, not the explicitly restricted CPGRAMS case APIs.

## Review gates

1. Obtain and record rights/privacy decisions before Mendeley or BBMP acquisition.
2. Verify Kaggle metadata and original government source before changing its status.
3. Ask the project owner to approve any ambiguous UP category-to-department mapping separately; no current source supplies authoritative UP routing ground truth.

## Phase 2 reevaluation

The [Mendeley rights and privacy review](MENDELEY_PHASE2.md) leaves the tweet archive disabled: the deposited CC BY 4.0 notice does not resolve full-text platform rights, deletion obligations or the bundled personal metadata. The [civic source review](CIVIC_PHASE2.md) identifies OpenCity/Janaagraha as a licensed narrative candidate but records HTTP 403 for bounded field-selected access. Kaggle's official metadata reports an uploader-selected MIT license and a roughly 1 GB archive, but no verified primary government provenance or schema. BBMP remains unsuitable for citizen narrative learning because its available fields are category descriptors and staff remarks. The [Nandi review](NANDI_PHASE2.md) documents the separate synthetic subset. No conditional source was downloaded.

## Phase 3 real narrative acquisition

An already-present normal CSV browser download from the official OpenCity/Janaagraha resource was verified against the publisher's MD5, schema and resource URL. Its 16,071 rows yielded a bounded 1,000-row field-minimized, redacted research extract; the full CSV containing addresses and coordinates was removed after checksum verification. The extract has 32 original source categories and is held under `privacy_status=pending_human_review`. See [official-source evidence](OPENCITY_PHASE3.md), [alternative-source audit](ALTERNATIVE_SOURCES_PHASE3.md), and [privacy protocol](PHASE3_PRIVACY_PROTOCOL.md). The Phase 2 DataStore HTTP 403 remains a historical API finding; the normal CSV download is a separate method. No Mendeley, BBMP or Kaggle record was acquired.
