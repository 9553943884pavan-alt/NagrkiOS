# Phase 5 local privacy-risk triage

The deterministic rules screened **1,000** redacted narratives without printing case text. They assigned **282 high**, **220 uncertain** and **498 low detected risk**. These are triage buckets, not privacy clearances; the 498 low-detected-risk records may still contain indirect identifiers. **0 exclusions were automatically recommended** by the narrow short/meaningless-text rule, and **0 human exclusions or approvals** exist. High risk includes prior redaction flags as well as residual or contextual cues; prior flags indicate what was removed, not necessarily what remains.

Aggregate overlapping evidence counts: possible indirect location 430, prior possible location 265, possible name 31, household context 20, possible person-name cue 5, unusual number 35, prior address 30, phone 19, email 1, handle 2, long number 5, PIN-like number 40 and URL 7. Counts overlap and do not represent distinct people or current unredacted identifiers. The Phase 4 direct-pattern scan found zero residual matches in ten pattern classes; the Phase 5 risk rules deliberately retain prior flags and add broader, less precise contextual cues. Any unresolved direct or indirect risk requires a human `revise`, `exclude` or `escalate` decision.

The redaction, pattern and context rules can miss uncommon names, private relationships, precise landmarks, code-mixed addresses and combinations of details. They can also over-flag common public places. The local reviewer sees machine codes alongside the redacted case; no machine risk level sets `identifiers_remaining=no`, a privacy approval, or training eligibility. The field-minimized source and generated case-level flags remain restricted and Git-ignored. The proposed 30-day retention target from 2026-11-20 still requires owner approval.
