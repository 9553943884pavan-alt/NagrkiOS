## Interpretation and next authorized route

No alternative in this review meets the simultaneous requirements of **primary provenance, public/authorized acquisition, genuine citizen narrative, original category labels and a privacy-minimized extraction path**. A README saying “real” or presenting a license badge is evidence of an uploader's claim, not proof that all rows are original citizen writing or that the uploader can relicense the source.

The strongest route was the official Janaagraha/OpenCity CSV, which was subsequently verified against the publisher's MD5 and minimized as recorded in `OPENCITY_PHASE3.md`. For any fresh export or inaccessible normal download, request a bounded field-minimized export from `data@janaagraha.org` (registry contact) or OpenCity support containing only source case ID, narrative, original category/subcategory and minimally needed dates/status. Preserve attribution and CC BY-SA obligations; do not ask for address, coordinate, reporter or staff fields. The project owner should approve retention and human privacy review before any records are called usable for training/evaluation.

No NagrikOS application, production database, NYC or Nandi collection was changed by this source review.
